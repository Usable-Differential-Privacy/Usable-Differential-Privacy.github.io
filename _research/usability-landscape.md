Usability challenges occur throughout the differential privacy lifecycle—from choosing tools and configuring parameters to interpreting results and explaining protections to others.

Our systematization of prior usability studies connects research on practical implementation with research on communicating differential privacy. It identifies recurring challenges, promising design practices, and open questions affecting developers, data analysts, and nontechnical stakeholders. These gaps include how to evaluate understanding, support privacy–utility decisions, communicate with different audiences, and account for organizational and legal contexts.

This synthesis provides a shared foundation for future research and helps position usability as an essential component of responsible differential privacy deployment.
