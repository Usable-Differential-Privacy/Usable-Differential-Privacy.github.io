Related work in the group studies how to build differential privacy systems that are correct, secure, and scalable.

This research investigates distributed differential privacy using secure multiparty computation. Its goals include developing scalable protocols that do not require a trusted central data curator, methods for evaluating those protocols at scale, and automated analyses and testing techniques for finding implementation errors. This work provides technical foundations for connecting understandable privacy claims to trustworthy systems.
