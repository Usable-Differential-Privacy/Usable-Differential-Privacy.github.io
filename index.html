University of Vermont · Research collaboration

Making differential privacy understandable, accountable, and practical.

Differential privacy offers rigorous protection for people whose data are used in analysis. We study how practitioners configure privacy systems, how organizations communicate their guarantees, and how different audiences understand the resulting protections.

About us

Connecting technical guarantees with human decisions

Usable Differential Privacy at UVM brings together researchers in differential privacy, human-computer interaction, security, formal methods, and responsible data use.

Led by faculty principal investigators Dr. Joseph Near and Dr. Yuanyuan Feng, our group examines the tools, explanations, and institutional practices needed to deploy differential privacy responsibly.
